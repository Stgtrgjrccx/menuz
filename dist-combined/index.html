Menuz | Autonomous Restaurant Operating System & Digital Dining Platform

Menuz is an autonomous dine-in operating system and digital dining platform for modern restaurants in Pune, India. It combines app-free multiplayer QR ordering, instant ESC/POS thermal KOT kitchen printing, a Google Review reputation engine, and centralized Master Admin HQ platform governance with 0% food commission.

Autonomous Dine-In Experience & Digital Table QR Ordering

Diners scan a table QR code with their native camera app on iOS or Android to immediately view interactive visual menus, customize dishes with dietary and spice preferences, and collaborate in real time with table companions in a synchronized multiplayer cart.

  • Zero App Downloads: Instant web app loading with 100% device compatibility on any mobile browser.
  • Multiplayer Table Sync: Friends at the same table add, modify, and review cart items simultaneously in real time.
  • Interactive Table Arcade: Built-in games (Klondike Solitaire, Culinary Crossword, Table Trivia Battle, Who Pays The Bill? roulette) while diners wait for food.
  • Direct Thermal KOT Routing: Orders transmit directly to 80mm thermal receipt printers over network IP (port 9100) or USB.
